NH Connect is the backbone of Novant Health's corporate operations. Our mission was to synthesize fragmented administrative tasks into a unified, high-performance ecosystem. We engineered a robust management system paired with an intuitive dashboard that serves both high-level administrators and daily operational staff across the network.
