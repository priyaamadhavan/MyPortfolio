About NH Connect

A comprehensive corporate administration system for Novant Health. Developing specialised mobile and admin applications. A robust management system featuring an intuitive dashboard to meet unique requirements for seamless administrative functionality.

The vision was to bridge the gap between high-level hospital administration and the ground-level patient experience through a unified, modular interface.

Context & Problem

NH Connect serves as the backbone for over 10,000 active patients in the US, acting as their primary digital gateway to Novant Health services.

Deep Dive: Simplifying Complexity

Why this works: We abstracted complex JSON properties into human-readable labels. Instead of margin-top: 24, users choose from "Relaxed," "Normal," or "Tight" presets. This ensures design consistency without needing CSS knowledge.

How complexity is handled: The targeting engine uses a "Natural Language Rule Builder." Users create rules like "Show 'Flu Shot Banner' IF 'Patient Age' is 'Greater than 65' AND 'Location' is 'Winston-Salem'."

Evolution & Iteration

Early prototypes focused heavily on the "infinite canvas" model. User testing revealed that non-designers felt overwhelmed by too much freedom. We pivoted to a **Structured Component Slotting** system which reduced the time-to-publish by 40%.

V1: Open Canvas

Low consistency, high errors, steep learning curve for clinicians.

V2: Slot-Based

High consistency, zero errors, 5-minute training time.
